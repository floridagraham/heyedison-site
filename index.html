02

A day with Edison

He is built for one founder running too many things at once. Here is a real day.

  1. 07:00

    The morning brief

    He reads your calendar and inbox and tells you what matters: two meetings, one approval waiting, three sales overnight.

  2. 09:00

    Hand a job to the team

    "Have the GlowBop team check the launch list." It opens as a chat in your Claude app. A soft chime when they report back.

  3. 11:00

    Talk over him

    He starts a long answer, you say "just the price". He stops mid-word, hears what you said and answers that.

  4. 14:00

    He asks first

    The shop team wants to spend $40 on an ad. An amber card shows the cost and the proof. Nothing happens until you tap.

  5. 18:00

    Run the house

    "Turn on the TV and open YouTube." He reads your smart home all day and only switches what you allow.

  6. 21:00

    He looks after himself

    "Are you OK?" He checks his voice, memory and disk, fixes what is safe and tells you in one sentence.

03

You always know what he is doing

No staring at a screen. Four quiet sounds tell you where he is, and you can cut in any time.

  • HEARSSpeech to text runs on your own computer. Brand names and your team's names are in his spelling list.
  • SPEAKSA warm British voice blended from local voices, with a little room and air on top. No voice company in the middle.
  • INTERRUPTStart talking and he stops in about 70 ms. Whatever he had not said yet is dropped, never acted on.
04

He asks first

Edison works for you, on your machine, with your accounts. These rules are built in, not bolted on.

APPROVE OR NOTHING

Spend, post, send, delete

Every one waits on a card with the cost and the proof. A Stop everything key halts all work at once.

VOICE STAYS LOCAL

Hearing and speech on your PC

Your voice is turned into text on your own computer. Only the thinking goes to your own Claude account.

ONLY YOUR SCREENS

Paired screens only

Commands work only from screens you paired: the laptop icon, or a scan on your tablet.

READ ONLY BY DEFAULT

Look, don't touch

Mail, calendar, files and smart home are read only until you say otherwise, one device or action at a time.
